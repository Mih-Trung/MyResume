## 🇬🇧 About me

I am **Nguyễn Minh Trung**, an undergraduate in **Management Information Systems** at the University of Economics and Law, VNU-HCM. I am interested in turning AI, data analysis, and quantitative thinking into useful, testable solutions grounded in real-world contexts. My current direction sits at the intersection of **AI/data applications**, **quantitative research**, and **workflow automation**. I enjoy decomposing problems, building Python prototypes, and validating the underlying logic, constraints, and outputs rather than stopping at an initial idea.

Selected experiences:

- Built a BILAB shift-scheduling tool with Python, Excel, and OR-Tools CP-SAT.
- Developed a Playwright/pytest automation-testing framework for LMS assignment-submission workflows using a Page Object Model structure.
- Contributed to in-progress research projects on AI agents in finance, dynamic pricing, symbolic-regression factor discovery, and market responses to ESG policy announcements.
- Participated in research and technology activities at BILAB, IEC.

I am open to learning and collaboration opportunities involving AI/data applications, quantitative research, automation, and information-systems problems.
